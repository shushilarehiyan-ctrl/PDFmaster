Privacy Policy

Transparency matters to us. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use PDFMaster and its PDF and image tools.

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

01. File Processing

Based on a review of the current website code, the PDF and image tools appear to process selected files within your browser.

We did not find direct file-upload requests to a PDFMaster server in the current JavaScript code. However, this is not a guarantee of complete privacy or a substitute for a technical network audit.

Please avoid using the website with highly confidential or sensitive documents.

02. Third-Party Services

PDFMaster currently loads certain software libraries from third-party content delivery networks (CDNs). These services may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser request details.

Their privacy practices are governed by their respective policies.

03. Cookies and Analytics

We have not confirmed the use of analytics, advertising cookies, or other tracking services in the current website implementation.

This policy may be updated if such services are introduced in the future.

04. Data Security

We aim to make PDFMaster useful and reliable. However, no website or internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Keep backup copies of important documents before using any file-processing tool.

05. Children's Privacy

PDFMaster is a general-purpose utility website. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

06. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, hosting arrangements, analytics, advertising, or contact methods change. Updated information will be published on this page.

07. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page.
